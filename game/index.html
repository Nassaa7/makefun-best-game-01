How to Play

Goal

Survive 10 waves of gloom creatures and defeat the boss, the Hollow Moth. If your health reaches zero, the run ends.

Controls

  • WASD / arrow keys — move
  • Mouse — aim
  • Hold left click (or F) — shoot lantern bolts
  • Space / Shift — dash (briefly invulnerable)
  • E — nova blast, clears space around you
  • P / Esc — pause  ·  M — mute

Getting stronger

Fallen foes drop green shards. Collect them to level up and choose one of three boons: more bolts, piercing light, thorns, lifesteal, and more. Kills in quick succession build a combo multiplier up to ×4. Elites and the boss drop pickups: hearts, wards, nova stars and lodestones.

Know your foes

  • Husk Beetle — slow and sturdy
  • Thorn Crawler — fast and fragile
  • Spore Floater — keeps its distance and shoots
  • Gloom Wisp — quick, weaving dashes
  • Ember Brute — telegraphs, then charges
  • The Hollow Moth — wave 10, two phases, calls wisps
